What is real in this demo?

Local simulation: the sample wallet, offline limit, payment receipt, and merchant view are stored only in this browser. They do not represent real money or real payments.

Connected services: if configured, the website can sign in, request a real demo allowance, open PayPal Sandbox checkout, and send receipts to your backend. The merchant is shown as settled only after the backend confirms it.

Phone connection: this website does not use Bluetooth or Android secure hardware. Its nearby payment animation is simulated. Use the Android app to demonstrate the phone flow.
